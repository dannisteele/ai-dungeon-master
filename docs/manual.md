# How to play

## Starting up
- Double-click **Dungeon Master** on the desktop. It starts everything and opens the game in your browser.
- Enter the table password the first time. The browser remembers it.
- The first page is **The Tavern**, with all your campaigns.
- **Check for Updates** on the desktop gets the newest version. It closes the game, backs up your games, updates, and starts the game again.

## Starting a campaign
- In The Tavern, press **New campaign**.
- **Choose a setting** picks a ready-made world. **Write your own** lets you describe where the story begins in your own words. **Prepared adventure** is a story with a plot already planned.
- **Solo** is just you. **Party: play together** lets several people join from their own phones or computers on the same Wi-Fi.
- **Kind of story**: **Grounded** is real life, with no magic or anything impossible. **Fantastical** allows magic and the impossible. Your own stories start as Grounded.
- Then press **Create campaign** and make your hero.

## Making a hero
- Give your hero a name and a short description (for example "skint warehouse worker").
- **Suggest a kit** has the Dungeon Master pack their bag with things that suit them. **Suggest another** tries again.
- Press **Begin as this hero**, then **Begin the adventure**.

## Playing
- Type what your hero does or says in the box at the bottom and press **Act**. Saying the actual words your hero speaks works best, in quotes.
- The Dungeon Master (the DM) then writes what happens. On this laptop each reply takes about a minute. The first reply after a break can take a few minutes while the DM wakes up.
- When something is risky, the DM may ask for a roll: press **Roll d20**. Higher is better.
- In a party game, everyone acts before the DM replies. **Go ahead without me** skips your turn.
- If the DM gets stuck ("lost in thought"), press **Try again** in the box at the bottom.
- If the whole party is knocked out, press **Revive** to carry on, or start a new campaign.

## Fixing the story when the DM gets it wrong
- **Edit** (under any DM reply) lets you rewrite that reply. The DM carries on from your version. Editing words doesn't change items or health.
- **Try again** (under the DM's latest reply) throws that reply away and has the DM write it again. Anything the reply gave, took or changed is undone first: items, health, people and where you are. Dice already rolled stay the same. It only works before anyone acts again.
- **Story so far** (called **Recap** on narrow screens) shows a summary of the whole story. Press **Edit** there to rewrite it, for example to remove something that shouldn't have happened. The DM follows your version from then on. The summary can take a moment to appear (longer the first time after starting up), and **Edit** works once it has.
- If the DM keeps repeating itself or arguing, press **Try again**, or **Edit** its reply to say what really happened.

## People you meet
- **People** (👥 on narrow screens) shows a card for everyone you've met, with the DM's description of each. The DM reads these to keep people in character.
- **Edit** a card to correct it, or add your own notes. The DM treats your notes as true.
- Once you've changed a description, the DM won't overwrite it. **Let the DM update this** hands it back.
- Under **Nearby**, tap someone, then **About**, to jump to their card.

## Settings during a game
- Open **⋯** (top right), then **⚙ Table settings**. Changes apply from the DM's next reply, and you can change them at any time.
- **Dice**: **Every risky moment**, **Only big moments** (just confrontations, big gambles and real danger), or **Off (story mode)**, where there are no dice and the DM decides what happens fairly.
- **Story**: **Grounded** or **Fantastical**.
- **Notes for the Dungeon Master**: tell the DM how you'd like the story told, for example "more dialogue", "keep it slow and cosy" or "no one dies". Press **Save notes**. The DM follows them every turn.

## Your hero's sheet
- The sheet shows health (HP), abilities and the pack. On narrow screens, press **Sheet**.
- Items the story gives or takes are added or removed automatically. New items are marked "new".
- When your hero levels up, tap an ability on the sheet to spend the point.

## Managing campaigns
- In The Tavern: **Continue** carries on a campaign, **Switch hero** plays as someone else, and **Rename** and **Delete** tidy the list. Deleting can't be undone.

## The Dungeon Master's brain
- In The Tavern, **Dungeon Master's brain** chooses the model. **Qwen 9B (quicker)** is the one that suits this laptop. **Qwen 27B** tells better stories but is too slow here.
- **Pack up for the night** frees the computer's memory when you're done. The next reply will take a little longer to start.

## Help and feedback
- **Help** (in The Tavern, and in **⋯** during a game) answers questions about the game and shows this manual.
- **Feedback** (in The Tavern, and **💬 Send feedback** in **⋯**) sends a bug report (**Something's wrong**) or an idea (**I've got an idea**) to the developer. Tick the box to include your story so far. Reports are public, so leave it unticked for anything private.

## Where things are kept
- Everything stays on this laptop: your stories are saved in a file in the game's **data** folder. Nothing is sent online except feedback you choose to send.
- The DM's knowledge of the world comes from the Qwen model itself, which runs on this laptop. It's much smaller than the AIs online, so it can lose track of things, which is what Edit and Try again are for.

## If something goes wrong
- **"The Dungeon Master is waking up"** for a long time: wait a few minutes. If nothing happens, close everything and double-click **Dungeon Master** again.
- The game page won't load: double-click **Dungeon Master** on the desktop again.
- Very slow replies: close other programs, especially browsers with lots of tabs, and check the brain is set to Qwen 9B.
